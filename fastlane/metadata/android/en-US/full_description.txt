Welcome to Mandate: The Long Game, the ultimate political survival and life simulation set in the vibrant, high-stakes Federal Republic of Naijaland. 

Hustle from the bustling streets of Oshodi and Mainland motor parks to the hallowed chambers of the Presidential Villa. Manage your core resource pillars—Influence, Wealth, and Family Loyalty—while navigating fuel subsidy removals, parallel BDC forex arbitrage, black tax family demands, and high-society Owambe parties.

KEY FEATURES:
👑 THREE PILLARS OF POWER: Balance Influence, Wealth, and Family Loyalty to survive political assassinations, scandals, and party ticket revocations.
💰 REALISTIC NAIJALAND ECONOMY: Manage physical cash, commercial bank savings, and USD domiciliary accounts against inflation and parallel market currency fluctuations.
🛡️ CLAN SOLIDARITY & BLACK TAX: Disburse welfare grants to ancestral compound elders, manage extended family financial pressures, and secure patriarchal blessings.
💥 IMMERSIVE AUDIO & DYNAMIC EVENTS: Procedural sound effects, breaking news tickers, and critical narrative flashpoints that test your conscience and ambition.
